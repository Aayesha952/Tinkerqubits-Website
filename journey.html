Our Journey

Empowering students and teachers with technology education to build a digitally literate future

2022

🚀
The foundation was registered and our journey of innovative, child-focused technology education began.

2023

📘
We introduced a child-centric curriculum, started pilot programs, and launched structured workbooks for students.

2024

💻
We expanded digital learning through Web-O-Block, a block-based coding platform that helps students design websites using HTML and CSS.

2025

💡
The Tech-Tribe initiative was launched to build a strong community of learners, creators, and future innovators
